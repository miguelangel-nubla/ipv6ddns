# Home Assistant add-on: ipv6ddns

DNS names for every device on your network, wherever its addresses move. It
finds every host on your LAN, IPv6 and IPv4, and keeps their names: at your DNS
provider, at your router, or answered by ipv6ddns itself as the LAN's DNS
server. No software on the devices, no static addresses.

[The documentation of the add-on](DOCS.md), and of
[ipv6ddns](https://github.com/miguelangel-nubla/ipv6ddns).
